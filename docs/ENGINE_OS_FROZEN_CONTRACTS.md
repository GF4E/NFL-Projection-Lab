## 2026 lifecycle

There was no validated market-free package when the lifecycle froze. A later eligible package starts a new immutable evidence stream at its actual activation time. To qualify as full-season shadow evidence, activation and complete Week 1 origin coverage must exist by Tuesday, September 8, 2026 at 7:30 a.m. Pacific. Otherwise the stream is permanently labeled `partial_season_shadow`.

In season, only values produced by frozen rolling-feature, state, availability, and declared coefficient-refit algorithms may advance. Features, transforms, hyperparameters, calibration, ensemble design, losses, thresholds, and missing-data behavior cannot change. Stale or partial inputs write a withholding and preserve the separately served last-good forecast with a stale label; they never create a copied or backfilled forecast.
