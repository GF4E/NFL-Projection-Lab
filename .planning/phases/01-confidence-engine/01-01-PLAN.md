# Confidence engine implementation plan

## Objective

Turn the research package into a background-first, point-in-time forecasting and evaluation spine without adding permanent complexity to the weekly board.

## Tasks

1. Implement immutable source, feature, forecast, scenario, evaluation, experiment, and human-adjustment contracts with strict as-of validation.
2. Implement market/SRS/QB-Elo baselines and a common joint-score interface for market-anchored, correlated-count, and possession-simulation candidates.
3. Implement derived mainline probabilities, scenario aggregation and fragility, proper distribution/probability scoring, calibration, coverage, and prequential diagnostics.
4. Persist accepted forecast/evaluation artifacts from background automation, register shadow/quarantined hypotheses, and expose read-only health data.
5. Add replay, leakage, stale-data, score-reconciliation, scenario, calibration, registry, and public-write-prohibition tests.
6. Verify lint, types, tests, production build, migration schema, and exact requirements.

## Non-goals

- No unvalidated hypothesis is promoted to the champion.
- No public ticket/money, referee, sentiment, or narrative trend enters a forecast.
- No browser visit may write forecast or research state.
- No human adjustment or selected outcome becomes a training row.
- No claim is made that licensed tracking or paid historical odds exist when they do not.

## Acceptance

- Every archived forecast rejects a source timestamp later than generation.
- All spread, total, moneyline, tie, and push probabilities reconcile to one normalized score distribution.
- The three candidate families emit the same outcome contract and can be scored on identical rows.
- Scenario branches retain their individual outputs and deterministically classify robust, fragile, or indeterminate.
- Every game forecast, not just selected edges, can be settled and scored prospectively.
- Experiment transitions are immutable and human adjustments remain explicitly excluded from training.
- Failed or partial input preserves the last good forecast and creates no mixed artifact.
