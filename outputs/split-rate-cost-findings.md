Private research update, September 5, 2026.

**The run stopped at its fixed cost checkpoint.** It took 3 minutes 28 seconds and peaked at 783 MiB, but the registered formula projected about 3 hours 33 minutes for the complete workload against a two-hour allowance. The 53 completed origins are preserved; all 224 remaining origins were left unstarted.

Historical data admission and the full 50-second capacity smoke passed. Independent numerical and chronology reviews verified the saved cost-stop evidence. Only 32 outer evaluation games were reached, so this run does not establish split-rate Elo's predictive accuracy. Its result remains `protocol_invalid`, and it will not be restarted.
