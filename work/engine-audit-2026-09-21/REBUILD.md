# Controlled rebuild plan

This is an audit recommendation, not a registration, promotion, queue reorder, or authority to change a gate. Preserve every existing forecast and experiment. Repository of record remains GF4E/NFL-Projection-Lab.

1. **Make production reliable.** Provide durable disk headroom (capacity increase needs spending authorization) and an explicit storage/freshness failure receipt. Track free bytes and write success before capture/grade/publication. Keep last-good output with its real timestamp when a job fails. Do not purge fits, grades, locks or experiment evidence to make space. Acceptance: a low-disk fixture cannot publish success or overwrite last-good data; host evidence shows healthy writes and adequate headroom.
2. **One immutable forecast bundle.** Bind point fit hash, training cutoff, feature/input hashes, residual calibration hash, numerical forecast, interval/probability convention and publication reference. Resolve artifacts by hash, not only a version label. A lineage mismatch must become a named failure, not an inherited default. Preserve an explicit parent mapping for historical forecasts. Acceptance: every number on a card/report traces to this bundle; exact current-score reproduction holds before any method change.
3. **One issuing/replay function and clock.** Use actual kickoff/issuance time and the governing cutoff/completed-game rule, including rescheduled games, throughout feature construction, rating updates, fitting and reporting. Wire three cutoffs to the approved production method; do not silently activate E1's rejected filter. Enumerate direct and downstream numerical changes against the frozen control. Acceptance: no early or duplicate use, same state inside a cutoff interval, Thursday-to-Sunday fixture, full 2016–2025 chronology checks, and no experiment artifact before Tuesday closeout publication. A chronological correction applying equally to models is logged as a correction.
4. **Uncertainty and reports from the same lineage.** Recompute historical calibration prospectively from that model's prior out-of-fold residuals. Decide and document what the expected team scores mean and derive corresponding margin/total/winner summaries coherently. Any numerical calibration change requires its own applicable registration/gate; a reference-label repair does not rewrite past intervals. Keep the proven paired-margin structure. Acceptance: mean/median semantics, interval and probability identities, calibration source hashes and future-data exclusion are executable checks. Current Season references match the current control; earlier versions remain labeled.
5. **First recommended method test: direct venue in point scores.** Register a minimal venue correction from prior data against the unchanged authoritative baseline, with the mandated gap sweep and hash before fitting. The observed -1.768 home-margin bias motivates this hypothesis; it does not guarantee a 1% team-MAE gain. Keep all other inputs/settings fixed and use the existing accuracy/coverage gate. Do not hand-add a generic football home advantage. Existing E-QB-DIRECT and other queue records remain unchanged pending an explicit priority decision.
6. **Earn additional complexity.** After the foundation is verified, test direct QB information, evolving team strength or other qualified features separately. Include prior-season league/venue means as permanent score-accuracy baselines. Report annual and pooled MAE, signed errors by home/away, dispersion, CRPS, interval scores and calibration; use paired and season-block uncertainty. Reject improvements that exist only on reused development seasons or depend on future inputs. Keep market-relative diagnostics separate and never use them for selection.

Stop conditions: paid infrastructure without authority; unresolved additions to parameters/candidates/gates/population; leakage; or failure to reproduce the authoritative control. None is bypassed by calling a change a rebuild. Replacing the entire algorithm at once would prevent attributing improvements and contradict the one-change cadence. Rebuilding its interfaces, provenance, tests and operational reliability can proceed in controlled pieces while the last qualified model remains the benchmark.

Confidence: high that this order addresses verified risks while preserving evidence; high means the diagnosis holds across seasons and obvious alternatives. Reduce to medium if independent production tracing contradicts the missing venue effect or lineage mismatch. The size of any accuracy gain is unknown.
