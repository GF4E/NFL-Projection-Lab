## Definition of at least 5% better

For the joint team-score target, retain **joint energy score** as the primary proper loss. For each eligible external comparator `j`, calculate on exactly the same games and forecast origins:

`gain_j = 1 - mean(energy_score_ours) / mean(energy_score_j)`

A benchmark loss of 10 and our loss of 9.5 means a 5% relative loss reduction. This does not mean five percentage points of win accuracy or a betting-return improvement. A positive finite benchmark denominator is required.

Before any external comparison is scored, freeze a benchmark register containing model names, versions, output targets, source and forecast timestamps, input availability, permitted evaluation rights, common-game population, minimum coverage and missing-forecast rules. Document how the available alternatives were surveyed and why any were excluded; do not select weak comparators or a favorable subset after seeing results. Include all admitted comparators in the claim and report the strongest one. “Available models on the internet” means this disclosed eligible set, not unobserved proprietary models or models without usable archives.

Admit a joint-score comparator only if its contemporaneous forecast supplies the required joint distribution, or a separately preregistered adapter defines an explicitly named model-plus-adapter comparison. Win probabilities, point spreads and deterministic score predictions require separate target-appropriate scorecards. They cannot silently substitute for joint distributions. No eligible external forecast archive is accepted by this goal update; the external 5% claim is currently **not yet testable**.

To establish the minimum advantage, require the **simultaneous 95% paired lower confidence bound to exceed 0.05 against every admitted comparator**, covering the strongest eligible model. Recompute the gain ratio within each resample, preserve week/season dependence and account for the registered challenger/comparison family. Freeze the exact inference design and sample-size/power plan before confirmation; do not reuse RF-02's 42-pair correction for a different comparison family. A 5% point estimate alone is insufficient.

Also require the registered calibration, interval coverage/width, secondary-loss noninferiority, season stability, failure, ablation, leakage and falsification gates, followed by independent prospective confirmation using forecasts preserved before outcomes.
