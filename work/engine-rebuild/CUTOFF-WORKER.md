# Scheduled cutoff worker

The owner-fenced scheduler gains a separate cutoff mode. It does not call entry sync, capture providers, source refresh, fitting or the projection publisher. It uses the same committed owner and exclusive dispatch lock as production. The only production-side writes are inactive state/configuration/operation evidence and the existing artifact publication transaction. The forecast issuer continues using its previous qualified path.

Configuration is immutable and pins the qualified state method reference, creation time and first cutoff strictly afterward. Weekly point refits remain separate. A missed state is not rebound to a later fit. A new method/code configuration needs a qualified migration; the worker cannot silently change its mathematics. The first real cutoff is prospective at installation, not a replay labeled live.

The service runs Friday, Monday and Tuesday at 06:00 America/Los_Angeles. A 60-second timer also checks for missed jobs; each invocation commits at most one cutoff in ascending order. No due cutoff means no state/operation write. A complete ten-minute budget must fit before a known capture window; otherwise the job defers before writing an attempt. The service has a 600-second ceiling, a 4 GiB memory cap, no swap allocation and one BLAS worker.

Before calculation, durable intent binds owner, configuration, cutoff, observation reference, attempt and original deadline. Interrupted execution first reconciles the immutable cutoff receipt. A committed state with a missing acknowledgment is acknowledged without another assimilation. An interrupted uncommitted calculation may retry at most three attempts inside its original ten-minute budget. Configuration, source integrity and local I/O errors latch FAILED_CLOSED. The timer does not keep attempting a latched failed operation. Operator repair must reconcile retained intent/state and use a reviewed compatible change; deleting receipts or blindly resetting a latch is not recovery.

Operation history is content-addressed and retained. The per-cutoff current operation is a derivative pointer to its latest hashed body. State, configuration, observations and all required sources are included in the scheduler publication allowlist. A real temporary Git publication test verifies their inclusion. No artifact-of-record deletion or retention change is introduced.

A completed numerical transaction can have incomplete inputs. A past game without a recorded final is explicitly FINAL_NOT_RECORDED; missing paired statistics retain their named shortfall. The committed operation says DEGRADED when any shortfall remains. Later arrivals update later cutoffs only. This is not permission to infer results or redate source availability.

The existing independent watchdog reads cutoff evidence and reports a failed operation, missing acknowledgment, absent configured timer, source shortfall or overdue cutoff. Missing receipts are overdue after 661 seconds: service ceiling 600 + recovery tick 60 + timer accuracy 1. It never starts a job. Host failure protection retains the previously disclosed dependence on the awake, connected Mac outside observer.
