# Executable expected-score acceptance checks

Previous goal turn PROGRESS. The existing independent FORECAST-CONTRACT audit already proves legacy limitations; do not regenerate it or invent a new statistical candidate. Add a read-only acceptance checker that distinguishes valid marginals, internally consistent expected scores, and an established joint score distribution. This is infrastructure for requirement 7, not a mean/support migration.

Gap sweep before implementation:
- Tier 1: linearity of expectation requires total mean = home mean + away mean and margin mean = home mean - away mean. A declared point mean must equal its distribution mean. A higher expected score need not have win probability above one half.
- Tier 1: validate finite nonnegative probability masses summing to one, nonnegative integer team/total score support, integer margin support, inclusive central intervals from the left-inverse CDF, nested 50/80 intervals, strict win/tie events and separately labeled tie-split scores. Contributions must add to their team points. No inference of independent teams or invented joint model.
- Tier 1: a supplied joint finite distribution can establish coherence only if its four derived marginals match the supplied marginals. Marginal moment agreement alone is insufficient. Missing joint evidence is a named shortfall, not evidence that the marginals are incompatible. Integer support is a necessary football-score condition, not a claim that all integers are equally plausible.
- Tier 2 REVIEW REQUESTED: use absolute 1e-10 arithmetic tolerance for probability/score comparisons and the existing 1e-14 CDF boundary convention. These numerical tolerances do not alter an experiment metric, statistical gate or forecast. Alternative exact floating equality would reject harmless summation differences. Report them explicitly and test counterexamples beyond tolerance.
- Tier 1: negative support and center/mean mismatch in a legacy forecast are returned as violations of the proposed expected-score contract, never repaired, clamped, recentered or silently relabeled. A strict assertion is available only for explicit future expected-score qualification. Current locked records and the fifty-file issuing closure remain unchanged.
- Tier 1: synthetic success tests qualify the checker, not any live model. Preserve all real E-CAL/venue/clock/reviewer prerequisites. No new candidate or fitted parameter is registered. An actual mean/support correction still needs its own approved registration.

Acceptance: tests include coherent joint scores, asymmetric mean/winner disagreement, distinct median/mean totals, superficially consistent but incompatible joint evidence, invalid support/probabilities, false expected-score labels, rounded intervals/contributions, input-order invariance and immutability. Record the required future integration point explicitly; do not claim an inactive checker enforces live release.
