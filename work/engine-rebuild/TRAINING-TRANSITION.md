## What the actual refitter now checks

`cutoff_pipeline.refit_recorded` recognizes this ledger, revalidates its method and exact historical cache, replays the 2026 source reconstruction, resolves later additions through their original immutable locked forecast/bundle/preparation, and joins labels only from the source snapshot frozen at the actual refit start. A ledger created at or after that start is unavailable. A missing cumulative closeout game stops the fit. Earlier games cannot be dropped or replaced through a child ledger. Provisional, unissued, changed, duplicate and outcome-contaminated inputs are rejected. The legacy training schema remains readable for retained old fixtures; it is not a route to activate this migration.

`append_locks` supports prospective additions from the actual publisher's final-eligible as-issued locks. Same-lock retry deduplicates; changed bytes fail.
