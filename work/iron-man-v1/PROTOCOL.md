# Iron Man Suit v1 / Week 2 operational record

The verbatim root spec and final confidence instruction govern this build. This is a new human-originated lane; the original runtime's 14 pinned files, distribution, Week 1 captures, locks, grades, and gate outcomes are unchanged. No autonomous Week 2 selection runs. Week 3–6 studies and the harvest queue remain deferred and unpromoted.

## Registered implementation definitions

- Home spread entry is a handicap (negative favors home); total is expected combined points. Market spread center is positive home margin. A zero gap has no inferred side and cannot create a wager. Quarter Kelly uses conditional non-push probability from the entered location, never confidence. The joint game and ticket caps are $100, weekly allocation $1,000, with $50 units. These are planned paper allocations, not claims of execution.
- The first submission per person/game is final. Separate private edit codes identify Gabe and Jarrett server-side. Neither can retrieve the partner's entry until both have submitted. The private SQLite insert enforces server cutoff and uniqueness atomically. Only locks enter the pick log. Absent two independent entries yields MISSED; no invented counterpart or probability.
- Monday 09:00 America/Los_Angeles is the registered EARLY snapshot, not proof of a sportsbook's literal first opening quote. Entries before it are PRE_OPEN; later entries POST_OPEN. EARLY locks only qualifying joint BET verdicts with absolute gap >2 and positive EV. Remaining markets lock at T75 from a qualifying T80 snapshot. A lock worker delayed over 60 seconds records MISSED. Stored capture processing can recover without a provider retry. Uncertain dispatch reservations remain charged.
- Per-person calibration populations remain separate (REGULAR and POSTSEASON). Fifty graded leans for that person enables both Briers, conflicts, calibration and descriptive better-predictor comparison. Eligibility counts are per person across populations; Brier summaries and per-level Beta estimates remain separate for each population. Pushes count toward graded-leans eligibility but are excluded from binary Brier and cover-rate denominators. Multi-tag rows overlap and are never summed as independent picks.
- Every entry freezes its confidence map/version. Until the first weekly posterior is available, it uses provisional v1. Monday weekly maps use Beta(20p,20(1-p)) plus that person's wins/losses at each confidence level; missing-level observations preserve the prior. New maps apply prospectively; first grades and prior locked probabilities never change. CONFLICT uses nearest mapped level to the number-implied conditional cover probability, ties to lower level, distance strictly greater than one. Below 50 no map-derived statistic is rendered. Confidence remains a stated ordinal input and the disagreement rule is unchanged.
- Descriptive ridge penalty 1, unpenalized intercept, separate offense/defense effects. Team-season weights are normalized over games: through Week 5 prior=(6-week)/5, current=(week-1)/5; current only from Week 6. No current-season games retains prior-only through Week 5 with PARTIAL status. QB metrics use available prior/current plays with the declared season weights. No fitted sensor changes a person's number.
- Success is nflverse EPA>0; explosives are rush >=10 or pass >=20 yards. Pressure is observed FTN participation `was_pressure`, not sack/hit inference. Current-season pressure is unavailable and prior-only coverage is stated. Run-block responsibility is not in participation, so remains unknown. Career starts are listed regular-season schedule QB starts from 1999 onward, as-of prior week, not most-attempts proxy. Depth chart QB identity is not an inactive report. Backup and non-QB injury statuses remain unknown where no qualified report exists.
- Momentum components are own-baseline z(YPP), z(points for), negative z(points against), averaged over the last four games. Scoring-composition TD return points exclude extra points. No luck index is asserted without the stability study. All base-rate and untested influence fields remain MEASURE/UNMEASURED.
- The site serves descriptive artifacts and performs deterministic pricing of a human entry using the published pinned integer PMF. It does not fit, choose an originating number, fetch provider quotes, or unlock late entries. Scheduled engine code alone freezes joint verdicts. Legacy hosting safeguards and migrations are preserved.
- Slip matching is a separate link export, requiring a unique Jarrett game/market/side and submission at or before placement. Ambiguous or missing placement stays unmatched. Original executed rows and grades are unchanged.
