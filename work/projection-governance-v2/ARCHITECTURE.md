## 0. Design principle
Treat the engine as a forecast system, structured the way numerical weather prediction is structured: a physical core that projects points from football inputs, an assimilation step that updates team state from new observations, an ensemble that turns uncertain inputs into a distribution, a statistical post-processing layer that corrects the core's bias and dispersion out-of-sample, and a verification suite that scores every forecast against climatology. No sportsbook line, consensus, price, or odds appears anywhere in the engine, the board, or the engine's grading. Sample size is 272 games a season; every component must justify its parameters against that. When in doubt, fewer parameters.

## 1. Core (physical model)
Keep the v1 core: baseline points per drive against opposing defense times expected drives, plus ridge-fit additive terms for the fifteen inputs in v1 section 2, with the QB, pressure-proxy, and kicker inputs active per the definitions already given. Target is actual team points. Fit rolling-origin by season, 2015 warmup, 2016 to 2025. Freeze settings after out-of-fold selection. The core is allowed to be biased and under-dispersed; the post-processor fixes that, the core does not. Elo handling is governed by Addendum 1 section A.

## 2. Assimilation (team state)
Replace the weekly full refit of team strength with a state-space update. Each team carries a latent strength vector (offense, defense) that evolves weekly: prior = last week's state plus process noise; observation = this week's game outcome with observation noise; posterior = Kalman update (Glickman and Stern 1998 structure). Preseason state = last season's final state shrunk toward the league mean; shrink fraction and process noise fit out-of-fold on 2016 to 2025. This replaces the linear Week 1 to 5 decay: the state converges toward current data as fast as the observation noise allows. Report the effective half-life implied by the fitted noise. Equations, noise, and cadence are specified in Addendum 1 section A. Gate: out-of-fold team MAE for Weeks 2 to 6 no worse than the v1 decay, and dispersion of projected team points at least 4.0.

## 3. Ensemble
Generate the projection distribution by perturbing the uncertain inputs, not by one residual curve. Members: sample team state from its posterior covariance; sample QB status from the depth chart and injury designation; sample the weather forecast from the forecast's own uncertainty; sample the core's residual from its out-of-fold distribution. 500 members per game, seeded, seed stored. The projected points, margin, total, winner probability, and all intervals come from the ensemble. Record spread-skill: ensemble spread versus realized error by week. Distributions are specified in Addendum 1 section B. Gate: 50 and 80 coverage within ±3 out-of-fold, and spread-skill correlation positive.

## 4. Post-processing (Model Output Statistics)
Fit an EMOS-style layer on out-of-fold core outputs: actual team points = a + b × core projection, with b free, plus a dispersion scale, fit rolling-origin on prior seasons only and applied to the current season. In Phase A the dispersion scale applies to the core's residual distribution; in Phase C it is refit on ensemble spread. This is the compression fix: if b exceeds 1 the core is under-dispersed and the layer stretches it; report b by season. Add one regime term: bias as a function of projected points band. Nothing else. The post-processor never sees the current season's outcomes before issuing. Fit method and bands are specified in Addendum 1 section C. Gate: out-of-fold team MAE improves over the raw core, and coverage stays inside ±3.

## 5. Weather
5.1 Data. Add from Open-Meteo at the T-80 pull: wind_speed_10m, wind_gusts_10m, wind_direction_10m, precipitation, precipitation_probability, temperature_2m, relative_humidity_2m, snowfall, at kickoff hour and as the mean and max across the 3-hour game window; 15-minute endpoint where available. Add config/stadiums.json with lat, lon, roof type and retractable policy by season, field azimuth in degrees, altitude, and surface by season, seeded from the Thompson Bliss stadium_coordinates.csv and stadium pages, every row sourced. Weather applies to outdoor and open-roof games only; retractable roofs default to closed when the game-day state is unknown and the unknown is flagged.
5.2 Fitting integrity. Fit weather terms on as-issued historical forecasts only: Open-Meteo Historical Forecast API from its documented start (about 2021) and Previous Runs API from January 2024. For 2016 to 2020, ERA5 reanalysis is used only in a separate mechanism study and labeled PROXY; it never enters a fitted weight that ships. Report weather coefficients fit on forecasts versus fit on reanalysis for the overlap years; they should agree within one standard error.
5.3 Features, in this order, each a separate out-of-fold test: (1) along-field wind = speed × |cos(wind direction minus field azimuth)| and crosswind = speed × |sin(wind direction minus field azimuth)|, replacing raw speed; (2) a piecewise-linear wind term with knots near 10 and 20 mph, as fallback and as a check that the 15 to 20 mph null in our data disappears; (3) gust excess = gusts minus sustained; (4) wind × offense pass tendency and along-wind × kicker range interactions; (5) precipitation amount and precipitation probability as continuous terms; (6) snow flag with a wide prior; (7) altitude term for Denver; (8) surface by season and a wet-grass interaction; (9) a per-stadium shielding factor, shrunk hard toward zero; (10) dome-built or warm-built offense playing in cold, as an interaction. Temperature enters only through the kicking sub-model and the heat interaction; it is not a scoring term on its own.
5.4 Structure. Weather enters as a multiplicative adjustment to points per drive applied after the core, not as additive dummies. Wind shifts the mean of the team-points distribution and does not widen the interval; precipitation and snow may widen it. This is a tested constraint: verify on out-of-fold data that residual standard deviation in windy games is not larger than in calm games, and report it.
5.5 Gates. Each feature ships only if it lowers out-of-fold team MAE on outdoor games with coverage inside ±3. Report the effect of along-field and crosswind versus raw speed on the 15 to 20 mph bucket specifically.

## 6. Baselines and verification
6.1 Baselines every forecast is scored against: climatology (league mean points per team, and last season's team mean), persistence (each team's last-four-game mean), and the v1 engine. Report skill score = 1 minus MSE of the engine over MSE of climatology, by week and season, for team points, margin, and total.
6.2 Probabilistic verification each week: CRPS for team points, margin, and total; PIT histogram for each; reliability diagram for the winner probability in ten bins; spread-skill plot; interval coverage at 50 and 80. All against actual scores only.
6.3 Predictability floor. Estimate the irreducible error of team points from a drive-level simulation of the same matchup under identical inputs, and from the residual variance of repeated matchups within a season. Report the floor beside the engine's MAE. Definition in Addendum 1 section D.
6.4 Lead-time skill. Score the Sunday-night sheet, the Friday refresh, and the T-75 issuance separately, so the skill curve by lead time is visible.
