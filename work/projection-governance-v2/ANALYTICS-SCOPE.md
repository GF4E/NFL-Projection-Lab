# Statistical analytics scope — binding September 20, 2026

The engine produces expected team totals. It is not a betting engine. Gabe's decisions and betting rules belong outside the statistical model.

Every new experiment is scored on actual-score accuracy: team-points MAE primary; margin MAE, total MAE, signed bias, projected/actual dispersion, interval coverage, CRPS and interval score supporting. No experiment is scored, gated, tuned or ranked on ATS, cover rate, over-under outcomes or any market-relative measure. Those measures cannot justify a model change. Prior completed experiments and exceptions remain historical evidence, not permission for new market-relative work.

The existing reference metric is DIAGNOSTIC ONLY: two compact report lines, CLOSE ATS and total, plus OPEN spreads where coverage allows. Each reports correct/scored counts and 95% intervals, pooled and by season; annual counts expand within the same line. The experiment-report diagnostic describes the deployed model only, never scores or ranks candidates against a line, and missing diagnostic evidence does not block an accuracy report. No diagnostic section, bucket ranking, break-even recommendations, strategy registration or forward test. OPEN totals remain INSUFFICIENT at 34.3% historical 2021–2025 coverage. References stay separate and do not enter any model input or optimization. Historical bucket reconciliation tolerance remains max(1.0 percentage points, one game); headlines allow 0.5 percentage points.

The 38-condition search is a user-supplied, unconfirmed registry finding only. It is not an edge, candidate, queued experiment or implementation specification. Its dead ends are not retested. Gabe's existing paper wind rule remains intact and separately labeled as an unconditional Under rule based on raw outcomes, not an engine-projection rule.

Accuracy priority: E-ELO-QB. VALUE is already supplied and implemented, including a scale fitted separately before each outer season and the oracle identity comparison. Preserve the completed accuracy rejection; active focus is review of that existing evidence, not another identical fit or a false missing-VALUE blocker. No market-relative result enters this work.

Confidence: near-total — this scope records the user's explicit instructions and the verified experiment status. Move down to high if source/queue identity or the recorded completed-run provenance is invalidated.
