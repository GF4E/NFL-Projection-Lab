# E1 constrained state conventions

Authority: Addendum 1 A, its A.1–A.8 supplement, and the subsequent replacement A.2. Governance keeps Elo in E1; removal remains E5. Exactly three optimized filter parameters, q, r, lambda. No comparative results were inspected while defining these operations.

State is [32 offenses, 32 defenses], in sorted canonical team order, PPD above the training-window league mean. C=I-11'/64. Every prediction, update and preseason transition projects mean and covariance; weekly Q=q*C. Actual observations are home and away scoring PPD net of the existing active non-strength offsets, with H rows o_home-d_away and o_away-d_home. This normalizes point noise by pregame expected drives so r has the requested PPD-squared units. The active control has calibration and Elo only: venue, QB, weather and kicking offsets are zero/inactive, and E1 does not activate them. Elo and the existing ridge calibration stay outside the strength filter in the unchanged core. The ridge calibration uses each candidate's own baseline and only past training rows.

Reference schedule: 32 virtual team-versus-league games per reference week. For each team i, H[2i] has +1 at o_i and -1/32 at every defense; H[2i+1] has +1/32 at every offense and -1 at d_i. The two observations are the team's scoring and its virtual opponent's scoring. Block R=r*[[1,rho],[rho,1]]. This symmetric reference has rank 63. Riccati starts at zero covariance, applies q*C then the simultaneous reference update, and stops at maximum absolute covariance change below 1e-10, cap 10,000. Nonconvergence blocks fitting. P0 is its posterior covariance. The component retention operator is I-KH; reported component gains are the offense and defense diagonals of KH, with half_life=log(.5)/log(1-gain). This is only an uninterrupted-reference-schedule approximation, not an added gate.

Preseason: mean=lambda*x; covariance=lambda^2*P+(1-lambda^2)*D*P0*D, projected through C, where D has sqrt(2) on both components of a flagged team and 1 otherwise. This PSD congruence specifies the cross-covariances of the doubled injection after P0 became nondiagonal. Unknown staff means false, explicitly reported. 2013 initializes at zero/P0; 2012 is an earlier calibration-training pilot only, never a scored gate season. Byes get prediction only. Missing scores never update. All forecasts for a week are computed before its first result update; the update becomes the next Tuesday's state. No in-week forecast refresh is implemented or activated by this experiment.

Rho estimation is not a search: run a training-only pilot with the first fixed starting triple (.01,2,.5), rho=0; take the Pearson correlation of paired, pre-slate one-step PPD errors; freeze that plug-in rho for the three likelihood optimizations. Undefined or singular correlation blocks the fold. Optimize q,r,lambda with L-BFGS-B and precisely the three specified starts and bounds; highest likelihood wins, exact ties to first start. Report rho as an additional estimated nuisance quantity, distinct from the three optimized parameters; report games/3 and games/4 to avoid hiding its estimation cost. No OOF MAE enters this fit.

k4/k8 change the blend used to estimate team scoring strength only, using completed current-season games n and n/(n+k). Expected drives and all non-strength core inputs retain their control values. Ridge groups calibration/Elo and penalty 10 stay fixed. Each annual scored fold uses only earlier training seasons. Each candidate's empirical uncertainty distribution uses its own paired residuals from the previous three calibration-only/OOF seasons. No E2 bias/dispersion fit is performed here. Point forecasts stay at core outputs; paired empirical residual samples supply intervals and probabilities.

Historical period is reused development evidence. Compare all three predeclared fixed challengers against identical linear-control games, report each result, and disclose selection. No hidden fourth challenger or parameter grid. The only parameter fitting is training-only; candidate-specific calibration is strictly prior-season. Gate: pooled 2016–2025 team MAE improvement at least 1%, plus pooled margin/total 50/80 coverage each within three percentage points of nominal. Annual and Weeks 1–4 results are reported. Resample four-week circular blocks within each season, 2,000 draws, fixed seed 20260916, keeping both team errors in each game together. Among numerically eligible candidates statistically indistinguishable by paired 95% improvement interval, prefer k4, then k8, then state-space.
