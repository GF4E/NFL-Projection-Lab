The literal starter versus primary passer discrepancy and wrong schedule identity are different defects. Buffalo's ceremonial start illustrates the first; Washington Week4 and Jets Week16 have primary evidence contradicting schedule identities. Keep the 585-game membership frozen while those records are adjudicated.

A post-lock modification may be harmless (migration or formatting), but this audit has not proved which content changed. Conservative qualification rejects that record as proof of pre-lock content; the original-publication alternative is shown in sensitivity. A later retrieval alone never triggers rejection. Current structured metadata is still not an immutable archive.

No positive finding of engine identification failure is possible without an actual engine-identity record. No causal valuation finding follows from an absent coefficient or subgroup error difference. Those limits do not invalidate historical public starter evidence that is independently qualified.

Quality inventory includes existing EPA/dropback, CPOE, career-start proxy and backup flag. No new fitted value or market value is introduced. Revised historical EPA reconstruction is stored for both quarterbacks as an investigation aid, with sample sizes, strict completed-game cutoffs and explicit unqualified vintage status. No quality band is scored. An audited historical EPA model release or equivalent pre-lock value archive would resolve this dimension.

Confidence: medium for these qualification decisions: they use authoritative sources but depend on defensible publication/identity conventions. Downgrade to low if archived revisions or official gamebooks contradict the currently qualified evidence.
