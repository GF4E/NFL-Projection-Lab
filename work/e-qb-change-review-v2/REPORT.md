## Identification versus valuation

The verified fit retains calibration and Elo, with names baseline, elo, elo_difference. It has no retained QB-identity or QB-value coefficient. A displayed or reconstructed QB therefore cannot establish a direct QB adjustment in this deployed fit. This proves absence of an explicit retained adjustment, not that it caused an error or that team-strength inputs encode no quarterback effects.

All actual historical engine-identity cells remain unobserved: correct, incorrect and identification-versus-value partitions cannot be measured from score-only replays. The ledger separately records the chart selector, not a full reconstructed production lock. No evidence establishes that a missing announcement was unknowable. No supported general causal diagnosis is available.

Quality inventory: existing EPA/dropback is expected points per dropback; CPOE is completion percentage above model expectation; career-start count and backup flag are experience/role proxies, not independent quality values. Past-game EPA measurements for both QBs are now stored with count and strict completion cutoff. They remain RECONSTRUCTED_REVISED_PBP: prior outcomes do not establish that a later EPA/CPOE model vintage was available at historical lock. No market substitute, new value model, zero fill or outcome-selected band was used. A dated historical model/value release or auditable prior-only model lineage is needed to qualify these values. This is a vintage gap, not an assertion that historical passing statistics were unavailable.
