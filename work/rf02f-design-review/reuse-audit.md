## Recommended seam

Use a new pure split-rate model module, a new small trajectory/selection bank, and a separate RF-02F controller. Preserve every original file. Do not expand the accepted RF-02C AST adapter: `scripts/research_score_compute_run.py:253 adapted_tree` verifies exactly three manifest/identity/smoke changes, and `:295 build_runner` expressly binds a computation-only scope. The original `research_score_run.py:193 run` also assumes four families, 40 series, 43 full settings and its old inference family. Replacing only configuration or monkey-patching global model bindings cannot express this new experiment honestly.

The narrow mean-model seam is `scripts/research_score_models.py:99 MeanTrajectory`, especially `_deliver` and `forecast_origin`. A factory should return the ORIGINAL `MeanTrajectory("E2", {"k": KS, "retention": R}, teams, original_variant)` directly for the nine ordinary tied candidates KS == KP; never pass their state through S/P arithmetic. A thin off-diagonal implementation can reuse the inherited origin protocol and mean construction while changing only the E2 delivery increments and explicitly validating new variant names. Keep internal E2 semantics where inherited code checks `family == "E2"`; serialize the new experiment family separately. Do not change a base instance's family label and thereby skip its absorbing-failure checks.

For an off-diagonal game, with raw original-origin residuals eM = eHome - eAway and eT = eHome + eAway, the O/D increments are:

- home offense: (KS*eM + KP*eT)/4; away offense: (-KS*eM + KP*eT)/4;
- home defense: (KS*eM - KP*eT)/4; away defense: (-KS*eM - KP*eT)/4.

Apply the original 2020 multiplier to both rates, accumulate the whole eligible-result batch, then center offense and defense independently over the same 32 franchises. Preserve common retention, delayed old-season delivery before offseason retention, repeated-season-gap exponentiation, initialization-only rows, raw stored expectations, deterministic shuffling and absorbing failures. `no_offense`/`no_defense` mask their corresponding O/D increments. `zero_strength` and `zero_scoring_level` instead set KS or KP to zero throughout that variant's own history; they are not aliases for either old O/D ablation, and an ordinary tied rate pair does not authorize delegation of these new zero-rate variants to unchanged full E2.

A new bank should follow `scripts/research_score_replay.py:79 TrajectoryBank`: each candidate and state-changing variant owns its complete from-2010 chronology, including warmup origins below the mapper's 64-game threshold. Never splice archived annually selected states, change a running engine's rates, or start an off-diagonal from an old E2 snapshot. Snapshots do not serialize the full `stored`, `delivered`, `last_origin` and permutation state needed to resume a trajectory. The two output-only variants continue to borrow the full trajectory: independence changes the law, and deterministic noise changes a copied forecast mean without entering its later learning residual.

The existing `SelectionHistory.add/select` algorithm is reusable behind a new explicit settings constructor: it uses precisely the previous two complete seasons, sorted `(season,game)` aggregation, <=0.005 native-failure eligibility, minimum energy and the declared 1e-8 tie rule. Its original constructor calls the hard-coded old `setting_grid`; a new registry is necessary. Freeze the nine original diagonal settings first in their original k-major/retention-minor order, then the eighteen off-diagonals in one explicit order. This retains diagonal preference within the tie tolerance and allows exact nine-diagonal selection equivalence. Select the full family once before its season's first origin; every ablation/negative control uses that choice and its own complete trajectory, with no variant retuning. No 2025 outcomes enter any 2013–2025 choice; 2025 remains exposed diagnostic evidence.
