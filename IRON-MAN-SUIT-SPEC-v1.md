IRON MAN SUIT v1. Read this whole message, then implement the Week 2 build at the bottom. This is a decision-support system: Gabe and Jarrett originate every number; the suit senses, prices, challenges, sizes, and grades. It never picks on its own in v1. Every field exists because it is a step in Gabe's process or because the research says that step needs a check. Evidence tiers: A peer-reviewed or replicated public finding; B practitioner consensus with data; C blog claim; own = measured on our data; MEASURE = shown with a sample size only after we compute it on 2016 to 2025, never asserted before.

SECTION 1. THE PRE-GAME SHEET. One sheet per game, in this order, each block showing the number, the ridge opponent-adjusted rank 1 to 32, and the market's implied view beside it where one exists. Data through Week 5 blends last season and this season with a declared linear decay; from Week 6 this season only.
Block 1, Venue: current-season home field advantage from our own data, split divisional vs non-divisional (nfelo model: 2.95 non-divisional, 1.59 divisional, 2000 to 2019; home win rate 53.6% since 2019; Benz, Bliss, Lopez 2024 JQAS, tier A), this team's home/road margin split.
Block 2, Divisional: flag; divisional games are closer (tier A via the HFA split); whether they are lower-scoring is MEASURE.
Block 3, Head-to-head continuity: last ten meetings with a continuity score per team = share of head coach, OC, DC, QB1, GM and roster still in place since that meeting; prior meetings weighted by continuity. Sources: nflverse schedules, rosters, depth charts; config/staff_history.json hand-seeded from Pro-Football-Reference, 2016 to 2026. Tier own, untested; no public model does this.
Block 4, Efficiency: points per drive, yards per play, EPA per play, success rate, offense and defense, opponent-adjusted (tier A as margin predictors).
Block 5, Kicking: FG% by distance band, season and career, kicker identity; regression flag when season rate is far above career on a small sample (MEASURE the stability).
Block 6, Matchups: rush EPA and success rate offense vs defense; pass EPA and CPOE vs pass defense; pressure rate generated vs allowed; explosive play rate (tier A: pass matchups predict margin better than rush). Plus a QB SUPPORT line: QB career starts, TE target share and run-block snap share, RB target share, QB EPA under pressure (own hypothesis, tag YOUNG_QB_SUPPORT). Plus a PRESSURE VS EXPLOSIVE line: defense pressure rate vs offense explosive rate and offense EPA when pressured (tier B, tag RUSH_VS_EXPLOSIVE).
Block 7, QB only: starter, EPA per dropback, CPOE, backup flag. Non-QB injuries shown small and never move a number (tier A: market prices non-QB injuries; QB changes move lines 3 to 7 points).
Block 8, Referee crew: crew identity, penalties per game, offensive vs defensive share, home vs road share, this crew vs league, last two seasons, sample size. Tier C. UNMEASURED label until the Week 5 study; cannot influence any number. Note on sheet: market reprices when crews post Tuesday or Wednesday.
Block 9, Momentum, roll index: last four games z-scores of yards per play, points scored, points allowed against the team's own blended baseline, combined; components shown. Base rates by index band from 2016 to 2025: next-week ATS rate and margin regression (MEASURE; regression to the mean is tier A). Eliminated-team flag from Week 12 with its historical ATS as underdog (MEASURE).
Block 10, Scoring composition and luck: red zone TD rate, points from defense and special teams, FG share of points, turnover margin, fumble recovery rate, close-game record; luck index = share of scoring from components with no year-to-year correlation (fumble recovery: Football Outsiders, Lopez 2013, tier A).

SECTION 2. OUTSIDE VIEW. Above the sheet, one sentence: the market's story ("PIT by 6, 41.5: low-scoring game Pittsburgh controls") and the base rate for the situation the game falls in (home dog off bye, road favorite short rest, divisional rematch) from our 2016 to 2025 data with sample size.

SECTION 3. YOUR NUMBER. Gabe and Jarrett each enter, independently, before seeing the other: spread, total, confidence 1 to 5, and tags from the fixed list QB_MATCHUP, COORDINATOR, TRENCHES, INJURY, SCHEME, SITUATIONAL, WEATHER, PRICE, YOUNG_QB_SUPPORT, RUSH_VS_EXPLOSIVE, MOMENTUM, CONTINUITY, REFEREE. The other's entry is hidden until both are in. The suit shows gap to market in points, cover probability and implied edge from the pinned empirical distribution, and quarter-Kelly size. Rule on sheet: under 1 point is a story, 1 to 2 is a lean, over 2 is a bet. Disagreement rule: opposite sides means PASS unless one confidence is 4 or 5 and the other is under 3; same side sizes from the lower confidence; passes logged as PASS_DISAGREE and graded.

SECTION 4. DEVIL'S ADVOCATE. Per entered number: (1) what must be true, generated from the tags; (2) what the line did, open to now, direction and size, agree or disagree with the entry; (3) what this tag has done for this person, from the feedback module, INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE below 20 graded leans.

SECTION 5. LOCK POINTS AND SIZING. Two locks. EARLY: Monday 09:00 PT capture at open; lock any game where the entered number differs from the opener by more than two points. LATE: the existing T-80 capture and T-75 lock for everything else. Numbers entered before the Monday capture count as EARLY; entered after seeing the opener are tagged POST_OPEN and graded separately. Both graded against the nflverse close and reported as EARLY and LATE with CLV (Szalkowski and Nelson: open and close equally accurate, tier A; early-market opportunity is a hypothesis this record tests). The next week's sheet renders Sunday 20:00 PT from that day's completed games, marked PARTIAL until Monday's nflverse update. Sizing: one unit = $50, build to $1,000 a week, quarter Kelly from the entered number vs the executed price, two-unit cap per ticket and per game. Jarrett's slips ingested as before and matched to the lean they came from. Weekly credit cap 75.

SECTION 6. FEEDBACK. Monday scorecard, by person and by tag: leans, W-L-P, rate with 95% interval, mean CLV, Brier score on the probability implied by confidence level, hit rate by confidence level. Population REGULAR and POSTSEASON reported separately, never blended. Model picks, price picks, paper rules, Jarrett executed, and human leans remain separate lines.

SECTION 7. HARD RULES. No block that is MEASURE or UNMEASURED may change a number until its study is logged in HARVEST.md as positive. No pick logic, coefficient, or distribution changes before the Week 4 review. The lock job never reads finals or closing lines; the grading job never reads pre-lock quotes. Every base rate on the sheet carries its sample size and the data window. Every sensor value carries its data hash. Existing chronology, immutability, credit accounting, and MISSED semantics stay in force. Autonomy grant from AGENTS.md applies; the only stops are spending beyond the credit cap, deleting files, and changing a registered gate.
